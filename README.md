# Aman Gupta: Growth Architect

Build a world-class premium personal brand website for Aman Gupta. This is not a freelancer portfolio and not an agency website. Position Aman as a Keynote Speaker, Business Growth Consultant, Marketing Strategist, Corporate Trainer, and AI Business Consultant.

Goals:
- Generate corporate training enquiries
- Generate keynote speaking invitations
- Generate consulting projects
- Generate media/podcast invitations
- Build authority and trust with decision-makers

Brand positioning:
- Name: Aman Gupta
- Primary identity: Keynote Speaker, Business Growth Consultant, Marketing Strategist
- Secondary identity: Corporate Trainer, AI Business Consultant
- Core positioning: Helping founders and organizations build stronger brands, better teams, and sustainable business growth through marketing, consumer psychology, AI, and business systems.

Audience:
- Business owners, CEOs, founders, directors, corporate HR/L&D teams, educational institutions, industry associations, large enterprises, and growing businesses.

Design direction:
- Premium, luxury, modern, minimal, bold, confident, executive, sophisticated, editorial
- Large typography, lots of white space, dark + light theme support
- Use subtle motion, polished interactions, rounded corners, no generic template look, no stock-portfolio vibe
- Avoid flashy gradients; use refined accents
- Aim for Apple-level polish
- Use Space Grotesk for headings and Inter for body
- Color palette: #0B1220 primary, #D4AF37 accent, white background, #F8F9FB secondary background, near-black text

Website structure:
1) Hero section with a large professional image, headline: 'Helping Businesses Build Brands That People Trust.' Subheadline: 'Business Growth Consultant, Keynote Speaker and Marketing Strategist helping organizations accelerate growth using marketing, consumer psychology, leadership and AI.' Primary CTA: Book a Consultation. Secondary CTA: Invite Me To Speak. Show trusted logos underneath.
2) Social proof section showing worked with: Amazon, OkayPlus, Kanodia College, Mahima Group, Tanishq Builder, Arena Group Brands.
3) About section written as a story, not a resume. Mention technical background, moved into sales, discovered marketing, built Arena Infosolution, created multiple successful ventures, believes marketing is psychology and AI amplifies human capability.
4) Numbers section with animated stats: 100+ speaking sessions, 200+ largest audience, 100+ team members built, multiple brands created, several industries served.
5) Expertise cards: Consumer Psychology, Business Growth, Digital Marketing, Leadership, Sales Psychology, Brand Strategy, Business Automation, AI for Business, Future of Marketing, Hiring & Team Building.
6) Why organizations invite Aman: strategic thinking, practical frameworks, real business experience, actionable sessions, modern AI knowledge, marketing psychology, interactive delivery.
7) Speaking section with timeline and sample keynote topics for corporate workshops, leadership sessions, business conferences, college keynotes, entrepreneur events, and corporate training.
8) Consulting section with cards for Business Growth Strategy, Brand Positioning, Marketing Consulting, AI Adoption, Leadership Advisory, Growth Systems. Include challenges, approach, and expected outcomes.
9) Brands created section in case-study style: Arena Infosolution, Arena Trainings, Arena Fincorp, Briwon Academy.
10) Testimonials carousel with placeholders for video and written testimonials from corporate clients, students, and business owners.
11) Gallery section for speaking photos, workshop photos, corporate sessions, networking, and stage moments.
12) Blog section for marketing, AI, business, leadership, consumer psychology, SEO.
13) Podcast section as a future feature.
14) FAQ section covering corporate training, speaking, consulting, pricing, travel, availability.
15) Contact section with strong CTA: 'Let's Build Something Meaningful Together.' Buttons for Book Consultation, Invite for Speaking, Corporate Training, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Email.
16) Editorial footer.

Copy style:
- Confident, premium, simple English, powerful storytelling, conversational, authority without arrogance, no clichés, no buzzwords.

Animations:
- Framer Motion, section reveals, number counters, hover cards, smooth scrolling, sticky navigation, elegant transitions.

SEO and technical requirements:
- Create SEO architecture for Home, About, Speaking, Consulting, Corporate Training, Blog, Resources, Contact
- Add schema for FAQ, Organization, Person, SpeakingEvent
- Add Open Graph, Twitter Cards, Breadcrumbs
- Responsive, accessible, lazy loaded images, modern code, reusable components, 95+ Lighthouse goal

Extra features:
- Sticky Book Consultation button
- Floating WhatsApp button
- Newsletter signup
- AI Chat Assistant placeholder
- Resource Library
- Download Speaker Profile PDF
- Download Corporate Brochure
- Calendar booking integration

Build it like it belongs to one of India's top business consultants and keynote speakers, not a freelancer portfolio. Every section should increase credibility and encourage decision-makers to book Aman Gupta for consulting, corporate training, or keynote speaking.
